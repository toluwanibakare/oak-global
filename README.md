# OAK Global - International Business Solutions

## About the Project

OAK Global is a professional web platform designed to help organizations achieve and maintain global standards for sustained success. We provide comprehensive business solutions across GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance) Advisory Services and Management Systems Services.

## What the Site Is About

OAK Global offers:

- **GRC Advisory Services** - Governance, Risk Management, Compliance Advisory, Board & Executive Risk Advisory, Strategic Risk Planning, and Enterprise Risk Framework Development
- **Management Systems Services** - ISO Excellence, Business Performance Management, Strategic Planning & Execution, Quality Management Systems, Process Optimization, Operational Excellence, and Change Management
- **Business Assessment Tool** - Free MSAT (Management Systems Assessment Tool) to evaluate your organization's maturity across key business areas
- **Expert Consulting** - Professional guidance and implementation support from experienced business consultants

## Website Pages

### 1. **Home (index.html)**
Landing page showcasing OAK Global's services, value proposition, and call-to-action buttons linking to services and assessment tool.

### 2. **About Us (about.html)**
Company information, mission, vision, and core values. Highlights the expertise and commitment to helping businesses achieve global standards.

### 3. **Services (services.html)**
Detailed catalog of all services organized into two main categories:
- GRC Advisory Services
- Management Systems Services
Each service includes description, features, and benefits.

### 4. **Assessment (assessment.html)**
Interactive business assessment tool where organizations can evaluate their maturity across different business areas:
- Addressing Risks and Opportunities
- The Business Context
- Leadership
- Supporting Resources
- Operations
- Evaluation and Improvement

Users receive personalized scores and recommendations via email.

### 5. **Contact (contact.html)**
Contact form for inquiries. Users can specify their service interests and get direct communication channel to the OAK Global team.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Vite
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: MySQL
- **Email**: Nodemailer (SMTP)
- **SEO**: Sitemap.xml, robots.txt, structured data (schema.org)
